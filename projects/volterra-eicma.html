SCHOOL FEATURE · NOVEMBER 2026

From School to Innovation.

Volterra students at EICMA 2026 — a field experience connecting Electrotechnics and Electronics education with the technologies shaping modern two-wheel mobility.

ITTS “VITO VOLTERRA” EICMA 2026 MILAN · ITALY

On Friday 6 November, the fourth-year classes 4F, 4G and 4H from the Electrotechnics and Electronics programme visited EICMA 2026 in Milan, the International Motorcycle and Bicycle Exhibition hosted at Fiera Milano Rho.

The visit offered students a direct view of an industrial sector where electrotechnical and automation skills are becoming increasingly advanced. Beyond the motorcycles and vehicles on display, the experience focused on understanding how technology has become central to the development of modern mobility.

Engineering beyond the classroom

Today’s motorcycles are not only mechanical systems. They are integrated platforms combining electronics, electrotechnics, sensors and automation. Electric motor control, batteries and energy monitoring, electronic control units, sensors, actuators, assisted braking and stability systems are all examples of technologies connected to the students’ daily studies.

The exhibition made it possible to recognise in an industrial context the principles explored in lessons and laboratories: the conversion and management of electrical energy, automatic control systems, signal acquisition and processing, and the interaction between electrical, electronic and mechanical components.

Technology, responsibility and future work

EICMA also introduced students to a sector in continuous transformation, where technological innovation, energy efficiency, safety and sustainability represent critical challenges for companies and future professionals. As an international reference point for the two-wheel industry, the event showed how the skills developed at school are applied, valued and transformed into the mobility solutions of tomorrow.

This school feature thanks Professor Matteo Marcon and the accompanying teachers for contributing to this new educational experience.
