02PROFILE / IDENTITY

One engineer. Multiple domains.

My work sits at the intersection of aerospace, systems engineering, software and AI. Below is the system architecture of how I think — each node is a domain, the lines are the connections I actually use.

I'm a final-year Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Padua with hands-on experience in CubeSat systems, structural engineering and ECSS-oriented development.

Alongside that, I build production software: full-stack web apps, REST APIs, event-driven integrations, and an active contribution to BEPI — a multi-tenant platform for CubeSat and SmallSat system engineering.

I care about interdisciplinary problems: closing the loop between physics, software, and the people who operate the system. AI and automation are tools I use every day — never the brand.
