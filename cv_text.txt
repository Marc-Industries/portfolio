PUBLICATIONS
2026 Simulations and Vibration Test Results for the AlbaSat STM

· Presented structural design and FEM-to-test correlation of the AlbaSat 2U CubeSat. FEM analysis performed in ANSYS Workbench 2025 R1 / SolidWorks; experimental testing at ESA ESEC-Galaxia.
· We contributed to the structural modelling and the numerical­experimental correlation of the AlbaSat 2U CubeSat Structural and Thermal Model (STM), with a specific focus on first-mode frequency compliance and validation of the finite element model against vibration test data collected on the electrodynamic shaker at ESA ESECGalaxia. In the project documentation, we reported how we built an ECSScompliant simplified STM model in SolidWorks and ANSYS Workbench 2025 R1, using bonded MPC contacts, deformable point masses and distributed mass adjustments to match subsystem inertias, together with launcher-representative boundary conditions reproducing the 3U deployer and 1U dummy configuration used during testing.
· We jointly documented the modal analysis results (first six natural frequencies, with the first mode at 347.56 Hz, above SSMS requirements) and the full test sequence (quasistatic loads, sine vibration, random vibration and resonance searches along all axes), explaining how we processed resonance search data from a network of triaxial accelerometers to track frequency and amplitude shifts across the campaign. In the paper, we described the FEMtotest correlation procedure, including mode identification across multiple sensors, computation of percentage differences between analytical and experimental frequencies (1% mismatch for most modes, 10% for one mode under further investigation) and the interpretation of local amplitude deviations as mechanical settling rather than structural damage, thereby supporting the structural qualification of the STM and increasing confidence in the structural model for the future Flight Model.
