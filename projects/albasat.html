LESSONS LEARNED
  • › Test-to-analysis correlation is only as good as the boundary conditions. Small shifts in fixture stiffness move the first modes by percent.
  • › ECSS review gates force you to write things down early — a feature, not a cost, when results don't match expectations.
  • › MATLAB post-processing on top of ANSYS exports is the most efficient way to produce the figures the paper committee expects.
