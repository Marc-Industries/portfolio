INSTANT TRANSLATE · AI LINGUISTICS
01
PROBLEM
Traditional translation tools are static and disruptive, requiring users to leave their current context to translate text, which breaks the flow of natural communication in chats, emails, and forms.
02
SOLUTION
Developed a non-intrusive Chrome extension that uses real-time semantic analysis and a "Suggestion-First" UX pattern. It integrates Gemini 1.5 Flash to provide predictive linguistic assistance directly within the DOM, preserving conversational tone and syntax.
03
TECHNOLOGIES
